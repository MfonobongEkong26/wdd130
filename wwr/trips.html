Our Rafting Trips

Choose from a variety of rafting experiences designed for families, groups, and adventure seekers. Our experienced guides are dedicated to providing safe, exciting, and memorable river adventures.

Los Dientes del Diablo

Experience an exciting journey through challenging rapids and beautiful river scenery. This adventure is ideal for guests looking for an exciting full-day rafting experience.

Duration: Full Day

Big Mallard

Big Mallard combines exciting rapids with scenic sections of the river. It is a great choice for groups who want a balanced mixture of adventure and relaxation.

Duration: Full Day

Half Day Adventure

Short on time? Our Half Day Adventure provides an exciting introduction to white water rafting without requiring a full day on the river.

Duration: Half Day

Rapid Rush

Rapid Rush is designed for guests who want a high-energy rafting experience. Prepare for exciting rapids, teamwork, and plenty of fun along the river.

Duration: Full Day

White Water Challenge

Take on some of our most challenging rapids with the White Water Challenge. This trip is recommended for adventurous guests who are ready for an exciting experience.

Duration: Full Day
